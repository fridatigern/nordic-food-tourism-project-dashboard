About the project

A strong legacy

The New Nordic Kitchen Manifesto (2004) reshaped how the world sees Nordic food, and Nordic chefs have collected Bocuse d'Or medals for two decades.

A fragmented picture

High-quality local and seasonal food experiences exist across the region, but they are promoted country by country, with no shared Nordic story.

A shared decision

During the Danish Presidency of the Nordic Council of Ministers in 2026, all eight Nordic NTOs agreed to build a longer-term collaboration with the food sector.
